Getting Started

Your first life

  1. Fate. Your base stats, bonus points, spirit root and three heritage offers are rolled first. You can reroll them a few times, then you choose a heritage.
  2. Childhood. Three story beats, told around what you rolled. Each card shows what it gives, and every choice in a stage is worth the same: they differ in which stats they favor and what else they bring, not in how much. A few are gambles with a range instead of fixed numbers. Picks are permanent, and your childhood survives death and ascension.
  3. Character. Spend your bonus points, pick a school and name yourself. Your school matters most: it fixes your weapon affinity, your five starting techniques, an always-on kit, and your primary element. See Schools.
  4. Cultivate. Insight fills layers, and nine layers cross a realm. Breakthroughs never fail. Bosses, tribulations and old age can stop you.

The loop that works

  • Fight actively at the edge of your realm. Kills pay insight and stones, and boost your Vitality regeneration.
  • Craft your realm's gear. Each realm's walls are tuned assuming realm-appropriate equipment. If a boss feels impossible, the answer is usually the Forge, not more layers.
  • Run the garden and market in the background. Herbs grow offline, the caravan's daily stock refreshes, and arrays stack small passive bonuses.
  • Join the sect early. Contribution buys real things, and rank is earned through promotion duels against an examiner who fights with your own school's techniques.

Three warnings

  • Death is mostly survivable, sometimes not. Early defeats in a realm are free; persistent ones start rolling permadeath. Tribulations three-strike you. Your Soul (the cross-life meta) always survives.
  • Elemental insight is one-way. Read the tooltips on the Elements tab before committing. Each element states exactly what every level buys before you pay. See Five Elements.
  • The demonic path is a real commitment. See Paths.
