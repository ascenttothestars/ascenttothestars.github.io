Mortal. Common clay. The dao is a rumor told in temple courtyards.

Qi Condensation. Qi enters the dantian. You are no longer mortal. You are not yet great.

Foundation Establishment. The dantian sings. Your foundation is laid in cold stone and golden wind.

Core Formation. Qi condenses in the dantian into a core of hot starlight. Its shape is yours alone.
