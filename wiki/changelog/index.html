Changelog 更新

Curated, player-facing. Not every internal version is listed.

v1.27.216

  • CLOUD SAVES. Your cultivators can now follow you across the browser, Android and Windows. In Config, press Create a sync code, then Use this code with the same code on your other devices. There is no account and nothing personal is stored; keep the code private, because it is the key to your cloud saves. Your music and other settings stay on each device.
  • The game syncs when it opens, every few minutes and when you leave, and Sync now forces it. If you played the same slot on two devices since they last synced, it asks which one to keep. It never overwrites a save on its own. Delete my cloud saves erases everything stored under your code.

v1.27.215

  • EVERY CALAMITY HAS ITS OWN SIGNATURE AND FINISHER NOW, at every realm: the boss’s two great moves, Unbound. They hit harder and for a bigger share of your maximum Vitality, leave a deeper Wound (healing -75% for 3 turns, from the finisher as well), and their bleeds, burns, chills, drains and silences are half again as strong and last a turn longer. The Calamities below Golden Core get these moves for the first time.

v1.27.214

  • GOLDEN CORE BOSSES FIGHT BACK. Every named boss from Golden Core up now has a signature move that hits for a share of your maximum Vitality, straight past your qi shield (your damage reduction still counts), and leaves you Wounded: half healing for 2 turns. Its finisher hits for a larger share and carries a status of its own: the Matriarch’s bleeds run deep, the Mirage Emperor and the War-Court Lord silence your techniques, the Moon-Eater drinks your qi, the Executioner admits no reply, and first light burns. Heavenscourge forms hit harder still, and Calamity hardest. Boss damage is up 20%, more for the two Golden Core bosses.
  • A BOSS CAN NO LONGER BE BURNED DOWN PAST ITS GUARD. From Golden Core up, damage over time now counts toward the most a boss can lose in one round, the same as your blows. Fast builds face a proper fight; slow ones are not affected.
  • STACKING DAMAGE OVER TIME IS FLAT NOW. Hellfire used to burn a share of a foe’s missing Vitality per stack, so it grew as the foe weakened and was far stronger against a boss’s huge pool than against anything else. Each stack now burns a set amount that grows with your realm, and still with your Golden Core quality, a broken foe and your damage-over-time bonuses. The same goes for a soul-broken foe’s damage over time and for Sovereign Parasite. Tribulations keep the old rule.
  • CRIT HAS A CURVE. Up to 50% crit chance counts in full. Above that each point is worth less, and 100% takes 150% from your gear and skills. Your stat chips show the chance you really have, with the raw total in the tooltip.
  • Tuned against real Golden Core builds: a strong Golden Core 1 cultivator should now win most normal boss fights with a good share of Vitality gone, about a third of Heavenscourge fights, and a Calamity only rarely.

v1.27.213

  • FROM GOLDEN CORE ON, EVERYTHING COSTS TEN TIMES THE INSIGHT. Layers, the breakthroughs into Nascent Soul and Spirit Severing, skills, manuals, Endless Path ranks, element levels 2 and 3, and Five Color are all ten times their old price. Nascent Soul and Spirit Severing keep the same proportion to Golden Core as before. The breakthrough into Golden Core is unchanged.
  • What you earn has not changed. A kill pays exactly what it did, so it is now a tenth as large a share of a layer: at Golden Core layer 2, one ordinary kill is about a quarter of a percent of the step. Rewards that pay a share of the step you are buying (a Blood Hunt charge, a Calamity, an inheritance, the harder bosses) grow with it. Cultivation speed is unchanged too, so idle progress at Golden Core and above is a tenth as fast.
  • Insight you have banked is kept. If you are partway through a Golden Core or later layer, the rest of it now costs ten times as much.

v1.27.212

  • A NEW LIFE IS ROLLED BEFORE ITS CHILDHOOD. Your stats, bonus points, spirit root and heritage offers now come first, on a new Fate step, with your rerolls. You choose your heritage there, and your childhood is told after it. Childhood stories written for your heritage are more likely to be offered.
  • CHILDHOOD CHOICES ARE SIDEGRADES NOW. Every choice in a stage is worth the same total: +2 stats at birth, +2 in childhood, +3 at coming of age. They differ in which stats they favor and in the passive or keepsake they bring. Each card shows exactly what it gives, and a gamble shows its range. The few childhood fates that ended a cultivator before play began are gone.

v1.27.211

  • THE WHOLE GAME HAS BEEN REWORDED. Every tooltip, combat line, description and changelog entry was edited to read more like a person wrote it: far fewer dashes and semicolons, shorter sentences, and no more stock phrasing. Nothing about how the game plays changed. Every number, name and effect is exactly as it was.
  • The Vitality bar’s tooltip said “Vitality vitality”. It now says what it means.

v1.27.210

  • AN AMBUSH CAN NO LONGER PAY MOST OF A LAYER. What an inquisitor or rival demon pays is now also measured against the step you are buying. Up to a tenth of that step it is paid in full. Between a tenth and a fifth, you get 60% of the extra. Past a fifth, very little more: an ambush worth half a step pays about 17%, and even the largest possible ambush only about 21%. At Golden Core layer 1, an 11.4 million ambush now pays 3.6 million. Later in a realm the steps are bigger, so the same ambush is trimmed less or not at all.
  • Ordinary kills are unchanged. A lost Blood Hunt ambush still costs exactly what the smallest win would pay, which this change lowers slightly at the first layer of a realm.

v1.27.209

  • UPLOAD SNAPSHOT AND UPLOAD FIGHT LOGS WORK AGAIN. The server they send to was shut down a month ago, so every upload since has failed. They now go to a new one. Copy and download were never affected.

v1.27.208

  • THE GATES TO NASCENT SOUL AND SPIRIT SEVERING ARE WALLS NOW. Breaking out of Core Formation has always cost far more than everything else in that realm put together: about five and a half times the other eight steps. The two gates after it cost only about half of their realm’s other steps. They now match Core Formation’s: breaking into Nascent Soul costs about 4.28 billion insight (was 438 million), and into Spirit Severing about 21.4 billion (was 2.19 billion). Insight you have already banked toward a gate is kept. Rewards that pay a share of the step you are buying (a Blood Hunt charge, a Calamity, an immortal’s inheritance) grow with the gate.

v1.27.207

  • AMBUSHES NO LONGER PAY A THIRD OF A STEP FOR FREE. Every inquisitor or rival demon you put down used to pay at least 5% of your whole realm’s insight, whatever your build. From Golden Core up, that guaranteed minimum was the entire payout: thousands of ordinary kills’ worth from one fight. The minimum is now 1.5% of the realm. Nothing else about an ambush changed: a build strong enough to out-earn the minimum still takes ten ordinary kills’ worth, as before.
  • The Blood Hunt’s price for a lost ambush was always “what the win would have paid at the least”, so it falls with it: 1.5% of the realm’s insight, down from 5%. The lifespan cost is unchanged.

v1.27.206

  • ALL THE MUSIC PLAYS AT ONE LEVEL NOW. The tracks were as much as thirteen decibels apart, so the volume lurched whenever one ended. Wuxia, for one, was far louder than everything around it. Each track is now brought to the same loudness. Loud ones come down exactly. The quietest old recordings come up as far as your music volume leaves room for: fully at the default setting or below, less if you have the slider at maximum.
  • TWO MORE BATTLE THEMES: Crane Over the Spears, led by a flute, and Three Cuts, in three beats to the bar.
  • FOUR NEW THEMES FOR ELITES AND UNNAMED BOSSES: A Tyrant in the Pass, Trial of a Hundred Blades, Heaven Does Not Blink and The Mountain Answers join that playlist.

v1.27.205

  • EVERY NAMED BOSS HAS ITS OWN MUSIC NOW, WRITTEN FOR IT. Each of the fourteen has a theme composed for this game and built around that boss: the Mine-Heart Golem’s is in three beats to the bar, anvil, anvil, crash; a bell tolls on every bar of the Bell-Tower Abbot’s; the Tide-Queen’s rolls over harp with the sea between phrases; the Sovereign of the First Dawn has the only theme in a major mode. The later bosses bring a choir.
  • HEAVENSCOURGE AND CALAMITY FIGHTS HAVE THEMES TOO. They used to share the general boss playlist. Each now plays its boss’s own tunes in a new arrangement: a Heavenscourge is lower, slower and heavier, and longer; a Calamity is the same tunes with nothing held back.
  • SIX NEW BATTLE THEMES in the same spirit (orchestra, war drums, zither and flute) join Wuxia, Wuxia II and Into the Wilds. Four older battle tracks step down.
  • This music is new and will keep being worked on. Credits for the instruments are in Config.

v1.27.204

  • THE CALAMITY BONUS CAP IS SOFT NOW, AND RISES. Your insight and cultivation bonuses used to stop dead at four times the floor. Past the cap they now still count, barely: each further ×1 of bonus adds one thousandth, so an enormous bonus edges a little beyond the cap instead of being thrown away. The cap is ×4 up to Golden Core, ×5 at Nascent Soul and ×6 at Spirit Severing.

v1.27.203

  • CALAMITY PAYOUTS NOW ANSWER TO YOUR BONUSES. The 2% of your step is the floor an unbuilt cultivator gets. Your insight and cultivation bonuses lift it through the same diminishing curve that governs every kill, so it climbs quickly at first and tops out at four times the floor. At the end of Core Formation that is about 343,000 for a bare character and around 1.3 million for a well-built one. The button’s tooltip shows your current multiplier.

v1.27.202

  • CALAMITY IS A BONUS, NOT A GATE. A Calamity now has three times its Heavenscourge’s Vitality (was twice) at the same half-again damage, and pays 2% of the step you are buying rather than 20%. That payout is a flat gift that no insight bonus multiplies, so a fifth of a layer for one fight was far too much. The free point of the boss’s element is unchanged.
  • Selling or breaking down your last copy of a weapon now forgets its tempering and its attunement. They used to wait for you to buy or find the same weapon again. A second copy still in your hands keeps them, and a boss weapon carries them through its stage upgrades, as it already carried refinement.

v1.27.201

  • WEAPON ATTUNEMENT. Eight essence changes a weapon’s elemental nature (Forge &rarr; Weapons). An attuned weapon deals +10% to foes of the element it overcomes, and its tempering breakthroughs are then paid in the new element’s essence.
  • TECHNIQUE RIDERS 附灵. Six essence imbues an active technique with one random bonus from that element’s pool (Loadout &rarr; Skills): Fire leans to damage, Water to cheaper casts, Earth to mending, Wood to qi returned, Metal to posture. One rider per technique; imbue again to reroll.
  • DISPATCH 出窍, from Nascent Soul. Your nascent soul can leave the body and gather at any site you have opened: four hours for eight of its reagent and two of its trophy. It works while you play and while you are away, one errand at a time.

v1.27.200

  • SECLUSION EVENTS, for the righteous. Come out of a seclusion of an hour or more and up to two things may have happened while you sat: a cache, a patch of herbs, a stele, a spring. The longer the seclusion (up to your window) the likelier they are, and what you did before going in tilts them: boss kills pull toward insight, a long stretch of fighting toward spoils.
  • DIGESTION, for the demonic. Every ambush you win by hand (not on auto, and not in the unseen Blood Hunt) has a 35% chance to give back 1&ndash;3% of your lifespan. It is the counterweight to what lost ambushes and lost hunts take, and it applies to Demon-Heart too.

v1.27.199

  • CALAMITY 劫. Every boss has a third form above Heavenscourge, open once you have bested that boss’s Heavenscourge: twice its pools and half again its damage. It holds a single charge that takes eight hours to return, and it must be fought by hand: auto-combat stands down.
  • A Calamity pays 20% of the step you are buying, whatever that step is: a layer, or the breakthrough at the end of a realm. A Calamity from a realm you have left pays 20% of one of that realm’s layers instead.
  • Each one also leaves a point of its element in you, free. If that element is already at your realm’s cap, it leaves five of its essence instead.

v1.27.198

  • WEAPON TEMPERING 淬炼. The furnace has a second job. Tempering is separate from refining: each pass takes an hour and adds +1&ndash;3% to one random stat on that weapon: strike, technique, crit, DOT, posture or spirit damage, max Vitality or qi barrier. Passes cost nothing but time.
  • Tempering comes in three stages of five passes. Moving to the next stage is a breakthrough, paid in the essence of the weapon’s element (10, then 25). A weapon with no element of its own uses your school’s.
  • One furnace: tempering and slow refinement cannot run together. Start it from Forge &rarr; Weapons, next to refining; the furnace row in the House shows the passes landing. An off-hand weapon gives its tempering at the usual three-quarters.

v1.27.197

  • THE ELEMENTAL CIRCLE DISTILS ESSENCES. Every element you hold a level in now produces its own essence (Ember, Tide, Loam, Verdant and Keen) while you are away, half a unit an hour for each level, across the same window your seclusion uses.
  • THE DASHED RING FINALLY MATTERS. An element also feeds a quarter of what it distils to the next one around the generating ring: cultivate Wood and Ember Essence trickles in too. The Elements tab shows what you hold and what each hour away is worth.
  • Essences are what weapon tempering breakthroughs, changing a weapon’s element and technique riders will be paid in. Those arrive in the next updates; the essences start banking now.

v1.27.196

  • ARRAY LIMIT BREAK 破限. An array at its realm cap can now be pushed one level past it, up to three times. It costs the usual stones and a development timer (4, 8, then 16 hours), and the timer only runs while you pay the upkeep.
  • The upkeep is your path’s own engine. The righteous give up 30% of their cultivation gain while they channel. The demonic lose 15% of max Vitality, stop resting naturally, and bleed 15% of their Vitality an hour. At a quarter Vitality the channelling stops by itself, so a heretic feeds an array by going out and fighting.
  • Channel and stop whenever you like; the timer keeps your progress, and it runs while you are away just as it does in play. Abandoning returns the stones but not the time. One array at a time.

v1.27.195

  • GRADED STAT PILLS give a flat extra, not a percentage: an Excellent stat pill adds 0.40 pips to each stat it raises and a Divine one 0.80, on top of the pill itself. The soft cap applies as it does to every pip, so that extra reads 0.20 past the first rung and 0.10 past the second.
  • GRADED LONGEVITY PILLS are worth more: +25% years at Excellent, +50% at Divine.
  • Slow-refined longevity pills now rise in price too: +25% for each one made, like the fast ones, but on their own count. Making pills slowly never raises the fast price, and crafting fast never raises the slow one. Cancelling a batch gives each unfinished pill back at the price it was charged.

v1.27.194

  • THE FURNACE 丹炉. Pills now come in three grades (Basic, Excellent and Divine), each about 10% stronger than the last. Crafting as before is fast refinement: instant, and always Basic. Slow refinement is new: a timed batch in the furnace, an hour per pill, that can come out Excellent or Divine.
  • REFINEMENT SKILL grows with every pill you make, fast or slow (pills far below your realm teach little). A slow pill is Excellent one time in five to begin with and every time by skill 5; past that, each level adds a 20% chance of Divine. Each level also takes 6% off the timer, and the new Cauldron-Tending Array takes off more.
  • Slow refinement pays the plain recipe. It sits outside the longevity-pill escalation entirely: it does not pay the rising price, and it does not raise it.
  • One furnace, one batch. Ingredients are paid when the batch starts, the furnace keeps working while you are away, and cancelling returns the ingredients for every pill not yet finished. Graded pills get their own Use buttons on the Cultivator tab.

v1.27.193

  • Blood Hunt ambushes: you still meet them at full strength wearing down 5% every twenty minutes, but never below 75% now, so late ambushes in a long hunt stay winnable.
  • A lost ambush now costs 4% of your lifespan (was 5% plus twenty years, and the twenty years hit early realms hardest). Its insight stake is unchanged.
  • A hunt switches itself to Avoid after 3 lost ambushes or 12.5% of your lifespan lost, whichever comes first (was 10%). Three lost ambushes alone now always fit under it.

v1.27.192

  • Blood Hunt rebalanced. Ordinary hunt fights now open at 75% strength (was 50%), still wearing down 5% every twenty minutes. At half strength even a fully developed cultivator lost most of them and spent the hunt resting.
  • Losing while you FACE ambushes now has a price in every fight: an ordinary fight lost costs 1&ndash;2% of your lifespan (a lost ambush still costs its insight and 5% + 20 years). While you AVOID ambushes, no lost fight ever costs lifespan.
  • Only lost AMBUSHES now count toward the three losses that switch a hunt to Avoid. Ordinary losses alone no longer flip it.
  • A safety budget: once a hunt has cost 10% of your lifespan, it switches itself to Avoid (which costs no lifespan) and tells you so. Choose Face to take the risk again, and the budget then counts afresh.

v1.27.191

  • Blood Hunt ambushes are fairer. A faced ambush now pits your FULL strength, still wearing down 5% every twenty minutes like every hunt fight, against an inquisitor 1.25× as strong (was: half strength against twice as strong). Ordinary hunt fights are unchanged. Before, even a fully developed Core Formation cultivator could not win them: halving your stats and doubling theirs took away the lifesteal sustain that makes ambushes winnable when you fight them yourself.

v1.27.190

  • Blood Hunt fix: the hunt’s weakening now cuts percentage damage reduction too (wards, boons, skills, Mist Draughts), as the panel always said. Before, only flat damage reduction was cut, so a ward kept its full strength while everything else was halved. At half strength, &minus;30% incoming damage is now &minus;15%.

v1.27.189

  • THE BLOOD HUNT 血猎. From Foundation Establishment on, a heretic can set a hunt loose on any ordinary hunting ground, from that ground’s card on the Journeys tab or from the new panel on the Cultivator tab. It keeps fighting on its own, game open or closed, at most one fight every thirty seconds, for an hour plus half an hour for every realm you have broken into. Then it waits for you to send it out again. It banks at most half of your current layer per charge (kills past that pay no insight) and then stops to recharge, for ten minutes plus two and a half for every realm (time spent resting counts toward it).
  • The hunter goes out weakened: every combat stat (Vitality, weapon power, qi, damage reduction, critical chance, dodge, lifesteal) starts at half and loses another 5% every twenty minutes, compounding, so it never reaches zero. Your base attributes are never touched. Kills pay their full insight; everything else they would drop (spirit stones, materials, gear, books, contribution) turns up on only one kill in ten.
  • LOSING IS REAL. Any lost fight leaves you at a quarter Vitality and stops the hunt until you have rested back to half, and that rest comes out of the hunt’s own time. A lost ambush also costs the insight a win would have paid and 5% of your lifespan plus twenty years. Three fights lost while facing ambushes switch the hunt to avoiding them, so it can never bleed you dry unnoticed. Old-age death follows your settings: with “pause before old-age death” on, the hunt stops one breath short and turns away.
  • AMBUSHES FIND THE HUNT TOO. They come at twice their usual strength. Or tell it to avoid them entirely, at the cost of half its insight. The choice sits on the Blood Hunt panel and takes effect from the next fight.
  • Only ordinary grounds can be hunted. Demonic grounds keep their ten ventures an hour. The righteous grow while away through seclusion; the hunt is the heretic’s way.
  • DEMONIC KILLS NOW HAVE A FLOOR THAT GROWS WITH THE REALM. From Golden Core on, every kill a heretic makes pays at least a fixed slice of that realm’s progress (a Demon-Heart a little more), so a lightly built heretic no longer falls off a cliff at the Golden Core. Nothing changes before Golden Core, and builds that already earn more per kill earn exactly what they did.

v1.27.188

  • A CARVED INSCRIPTION CAN NO LONGER BE SOLD OUT FROM UNDER YOUR SKIN. The caravan and the Disassembly tab offered every inscription you owned, including the copy carved into your flesh, so you could take the stones or the essence for it and keep the carving’s full effect. Both lists, and every bulk button, now hold back one copy per carving. Spares beyond that are still yours to sell.
  • The same copy also can no longer be slotted into your weapon while it is carved. One inscription was able to grant its effect twice.
  • Fixed the other direction too: owning two of an inscription and slotting one into your weapon used to refuse to carve the second, as if the slotted copy were still loose in your pack. It carves now.

v1.27.187

  • SECLUSION 闭关. Time away now grows with you: two hours to start, an hour for every realm you break into, and ten minutes for every layer you have ever climbed, so a breakthrough only ever lengthens it. Core Formation layer 5 sits at about eight and a half hours; Spirit Severing reaches sixteen. Dao Heart lengthens it further, +2% per point above 10, up to double. (Cultivation points you can respec don’t count. Only Dao Heart you are committed to does.)
  • NO MORE HARD STOP. Past the window your cultivation fades to nothing over the next half-window instead of halting, worth an extra quarter-window to anyone who overstays.
  • BURN LIFESPAN FOR MORE. A new Seclusion panel on the Cultivator tab lets you trade lifespan for time: 10 years per extra hour, charged only for hours you actually spend past the window, and stopping at the breakpoint you choose: 75%, 50% or 40% of your lifespan left. Every breakpoint leaves you able to survive at least one failed tribulation; the panel says how many.
  • The window now applies however you leave. It used to bind only players who closed the game: switching away from the app or leaving a background tab open credited the whole absence at full rate, and meditating at a site in the background aged you for every hour of it. Hidden time now settles as one seclusion when you come back, with the same report a fresh start shows.
  • Returning to a fight you left open no longer throws your absence away. It is credited as seclusion, and the report waits until you are home.
  • Demon-Heart does not sit in seclusion, and no longer gets a 0-insight “you emerge from cultivation” banner.

v1.27.186

  • THE DROWNED MOON CULT LAYS BLACK ICE. The demonic Moonlit Water path no longer applies plain Chill. Every technique hit lays Black Ice instead, which stacks to five. Each stack saps 6% of the foe’s outgoing damage (the old -30% at a full lattice), and freezes their qi as well: meditation recovers 14% less per stack and their techniques cost 12% more per stack. A fully iced caster gets roughly five casts where it used to get twelve. It has to swing plainly instead, and those swings are down 30% too.
  • FOUR NEW HERETICAL ARTS around it: Rime Lattice (three thrusts, three stacks, or four with Deep Cold’s own), Glacier Calving (consume every stack for +55% each), Drink the Cold (+20% per stack, and each stack returns 2.5% max Vitality and 3% max qi; it eats the ice down to two and leaves them holding), and the Hoarfrost Doctrine passive (35% of your plain strikes lay a stack). Drowning Grasp now lays two stacks across the field. Shatter the Frozen reads Black Ice for a heretic, so the old finisher still fires.
  • The Loadout screen now shows your school passive’s DEMONIC twist as well as the base text. It used to show only the righteous line, whichever path you walked.

v1.27.185

  • EVERY ELEMENT NOW TELLS YOU WHAT IT DOES BEFORE YOU BUY IT. v1.27.152

v1.27.151

  • Combat action tooltips: the explanation of where the damage number comes from is gone, replaced with your crit chance. Tooltips across the game have been cut down to what you can act on. The Vitality hover text alone dropped from a paragraph to a line.

v1.27.150

  • The damage number on your action buttons is now the real one. It was being worked out by a separate, much shorter calculation that stopped after your weapon and school and ignored everything else your build does: cultivator might, body cultivation, weapon refinement, elemental matchups, the demonic bonus, turn-by-turn ramps, and the execute bonuses for breaking a foe’s stance or soul. The more you had invested, the more wrong it got: roughly a quarter low at Qi Condensation and up to four times low at Golden Core.
  • It now runs the exact same code the strike itself runs, against the foe you are actually facing. It accounts for their qi barrier and defences, and it moves as the fight moves, climbing as you pile on debuffs, tear down a spirit, or shatter a stance. The tooltip breaks out how much lands on Vitality versus the barrier. Critical strikes are still not included, since they depend on a roll.
  • The Strike button was wrong the same way: it advertised bare weapon damage and ignored your strike bonuses (and, for a Demon-Heart, the second strike entirely). It is now accurate too.

v1.27.149

v1.27.148

v1.27.147
