Paths: righteous and demonic

Righteous 正道

The default road. Sect standing, market access, tribulations only at realm boundaries. It is slower and steadier, and nothing hunts you. Time away pays through seclusion: your cultivation keeps running while you're gone.

Demonic 魔道

Faster and stronger: more stats, richer kill insight, demon grounds to hunt in, and each school's kit twisted into a crueler shape. Cultivation crawls (a Demon-Heart doesn't cultivate at all), so a heretic grows by killing, and from Foundation Establishment even while away: the Blood Hunt keeps fighting on its own for a while after you leave, weakened, light on loot, and dangerous to lose. The price:

  • A tribulation at every breakthrough, not just at realm boundaries. Tribulations are tuned to be able to kill you.
  • Inquisitors and hunters: the righteous world comes after you.
  • Sect life changes: heretical arts, enforcer work, different doors open and close.

The path suits players who want the game leaning on them. Demon-Heart Palace is the extreme case: the strongest school in the game and the riskiest, built around self-damage, Hellfire, and detonation windows.

Body tempering

Open on either path: the quench/temper line trades resources for permanent flesh. It is easy to miss, so look for it once you can afford to spend Vitality on purpose.
