Armor Sets 套装

Matching pieces grant bonuses at 2 and 4 equipped. An Ascendant piece also counts toward the base set it was forged from (upgrading never loses a threshold), and where Ascendant pieces earn a tier outright the Ascendant bonus replaces the base one.
