Ascent to the Stars 步步登天

A xianxia incremental cultivator. Start as a mortal with a handful of stat points and a freshly rolled childhood, and end, if the heavens permit, somewhere far above Spirit Severing. Runs in any browser, on Android, and on Windows, all from a single file that updates itself.

Play it

  • Browser, Windows, Android: downloads are on Updates and Platforms, along with how updates reach each platform (automatically everywhere after the first install).
  • New? Start with Getting Started.

Reference

The reference pages are regenerated from the game's own data tables every release, so the numbers you read here are the numbers the game runs.

  • Realms: the cultivation ladder
  • Schools: all fifteen, with their kits and techniques
  • Five Elements: kits, signatures and the overcoming cycle
  • Armor Sets: every set bonus and its Five Elements vector
  • Bosses: mechanics and spoils
  • Changelog: what changed, in player terms

Choosing a road

Paths covers the righteous and demonic paths. The demonic road is faster and stronger, and the heavens notice.
