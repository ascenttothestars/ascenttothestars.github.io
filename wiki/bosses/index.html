Bosses 魔头

Bosses do not scale with you. Each is anchored to its realm, so you either outgrow the wall or out-build it. Heavenscourge (hard) rites reward the Ascendant set line.

Every boss has two harder forms: Heavenscourge (the Ascendant set line) and, once that has fallen, Calamity, fought by hand on a long recharge for a share of the step you are climbing and a point of its element.

Bell-Tower Abbot (Foundation Establishment)

Nine echoes, nine judgments. The tower only rings for visitors worth measuring.

Mechanic: Guards 70% of incoming damage until the 9th toll, then the guard collapses. On that toll a soul-broken challenger dies outright. Shattering its stance drops the guard and restarts the count from one.

Spoils: set Nine-Echo Vestments 九响衣 · weapon refine Resonant Casting (spirit damage + posture)
