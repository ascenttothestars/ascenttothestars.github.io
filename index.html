What is it? 修仙

An incremental game about a cultivator's long climb. Breakthroughs never fail, so the walls you meet are the bosses, the tribulations and your own lifespan.

  • A fate, then a childhood

    Each life starts by rolling your stats, spirit root and heritage, then tells three childhood story beats around what you rolled. Your choices are permanent and follow you through death.

  • Fifteen schools

    From the Azure Sword Sect to Demon Heart Palace, each school sets your weapon, five starting techniques, an always-on kit and your primary element.

  • The Five Elements

    Metal, Wood, Water, Fire and Earth overcome one another in a cycle. Cultivating an element grants its kit and signature, and specializing is a bet.

  • Bosses, Heavenscourge, Calamity

    Fourteen named bosses are anchored to their realms, each with its own spoils and its own composed theme. Each has two harder forms: Heavenscourge, and once that falls, Calamity.

  • Righteous or demonic

    The righteous road is steady and keeps cultivating in seclusion while you're away. The demonic road is faster and stronger, and brings a tribulation at every breakthrough and hunters at your door.

  • A life beyond the battle

    Forge your realm's gear and chase armor sets, tend a garden that grows while you're offline, trade with the market caravan, and rise through your sect in promotion duels.

Cloud saves

Cloud Saves keep your cultivators in step across the browser, Android and Windows. In the game's Config tab, press Create a sync code, then enter the same code with Use this code on your other devices.

There's no account and nothing personal is stored. The code is the only key to your cloud saves, so keep it private. Your music and other settings stay on each device.

The game syncs when it opens, every few minutes and when you leave; Sync now forces it. If you played the same slot on two devices in between, it asks which one to keep and never overwrites a save on its own. Delete my cloud saves erases everything stored under your code.

Already playing?

Browsers keep saves per web address, so where you played decides where your cultivator lives.

Where your saves are

If you played at ascenttothestars.github.io before the game moved to /game/, your saves are already here: it's the same address, so they carry over.

The Android and Windows apps keep their saves inside the app.

Move a save with a Cultivator Snapshot

  1. Where your save is now, open the Config tab and find Cultivator Snapshot (it shows while a cultivator is alive and playing). Choose Copy Snapshot, or Save as File.
  2. Open the game where you want to play, go to Config, then Load / Transfer a Save.
  3. Paste the snapshot and press Load Save (or use Load from File), then tap once more to confirm.

The snapshot replaces the cultivator in the active save slot, so export that one first if you want to keep it. Souls and prestige come across; this device's own settings stay as they are. It moves one slot at a time, and works between the browser and the apps. Where a file option isn't available, copy and paste the text instead.

Or link your devices with Cloud Saves (above), and they stay in step on their own.
